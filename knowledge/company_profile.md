## Who we are
- Workforce Training & Development Ltd, Companies House no. 05052803
- UK apprenticeship training provider with over 22 years of experience
- Headquarters: Aegon House, 13 Lanark Square, Canary Wharf, London E14 9QD
- Delivers nationwide across England, online or on-site
- Started in the food and meat industry; now covers marketing, digital and IT, business,
  food and drink, butchery, AI automation, and engineering and manufacturing
- Levels 2 to 6

## Credentials
- On the DfE Register of Apprenticeship Training Providers (RoATP)
- Assessed by Ofsted as meeting the Expected Standard
- ISO 9001 certified
- Every programme follows a nationally recognised standard and ends with an independent end-point assessment

## What makes us different
- Tutors come from the trade they teach (e.g. digital practitioners for data and cyber roles)
- Every enquiry starts with a skills scan against the occupational standard, so training fits the actual role
- Programmes are 95–100% government-funded and we handle the funding paperwork
- Enrolment every month; enquiry to enrolment typically takes a few weeks
- Off-the-job hours planned around real shift patterns
- A named point of contact for each employer
- Works for one employee or an entire workforce

## Funding facts (as stated on the website)
- Funded through the Growth and Skills Levy; levy and non-levy options
- Non-levy employers contribute 5% for older apprentices
- Under the 2026 reforms, apprentices aged 16–24 are fully funded for every employer from August 2026
- Free consultation includes a no-obligation funding check
