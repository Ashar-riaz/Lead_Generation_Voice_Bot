ABOUT US

# Over 22 Years of Workforce Development Excellence

We're a RoATP-approved apprenticeship training provider that turns everyday roles into recognised qualifications — helping employers close skills gaps and helping individuals build careers. Eight industries, every level from 2 to 6, taught by tutors from the trade itself, delivered nationwide.

[Request a skills assessment](https://www.wtd.org.uk/contact/)

## Our Story

Workforce Training & Development (WTD) has been supporting learners and employers for over 22 years. We specialise in delivering apprenticeship and professional training programmes that bridge the gap between education and industry.

Our mission is simple: to provide meaningful learning experiences that improve careers, strengthen businesses, and contribute to economic growth throughout the UK.

By combining sector expertise, personalised support, and innovative delivery methods, we help learners achieve their goals while enabling employers to build skilled, future-ready workforces.

Our Vision

## To become the UK's most trusted apprenticeship and workforce development provider.

We want every business we work with to see training not as a cost, but as the fastest route to a stronger team — and every learner to leave us more capable, more confident and more employable than the day they started. Getting there means raising the standard of what an apprenticeship can be, and being the name employers recommend to one another.

That vision rests on four commitments that shape every decision we make:

**Support**

Guide every learner through a training experience that genuinely advances their career, not just their certificate count.

**Develop**

Shape each programme around the needs of our employer partners, so the training answers a real business problem.

**Inspire**

Show employers what good training does for capability, quality and retention — and make it easy to say yes.

**Encourage**

Push every apprentice toward the best possible outcome, with the support to actually get there.

KNOW WHO WE ARE

## About our company

Workforce Training & Development Ltd (Companies House no. 05052803) has been delivering apprenticeships and professional development for 22 years from our headquarters at Aegon House in Canary Wharf. We began in the food and meat industry — a demanding, hands-on sector where a mistake on the floor costs real money — and that grounding in real workplaces still defines how we teach today.

From those roots we've grown into seven categories: marketing, digital and IT, business, food and drink, butchery, AI automation, and engineering and manufacturing. We deliver nationwide, every programme follows a nationally recognised standard and finishes with an independent end-point assessment, and training is funded through the Growth and Skills Levy — our funding pages set out exactly what that means for levy and non-levy employers.

What genuinely sets us apart is who does the teaching. We match apprentices with tutors from their own trade — a meat-industry specialist for the Butcher standard, a digital practitioner for data and cyber roles — so guidance comes from someone who has done the job, not from a generic curriculum. For the employer that means training built around the occupational standard and the role you actually need filled; for the apprentice it means the person reviewing your progress understands the pressures of your working week.

## Key Benefits

Everything you need to train, upskill and grow — with support at every step.

### Industry Experienced Tutors

Learn from tutors who've worked in the sector themselves. They bring real-world context to every session, so what you learn maps directly to what you do at work.

### Flexible Online & Workplace Learning

Study in a way that fits around your job. Live online sessions, on-demand resources and workplace-based assessment mean progress never has to stop for a busy week.

### Monthly Enrolment Opportunities

No long waits for the next intake. With start dates every month, you or your team can begin as soon as you're ready.

### Dedicated Employer Support

A named point of contact guides you through onboarding, progress reviews and reporting — keeping paperwork light and your team's development on track.

### Government Funding Guidance

We'll help you understand what funding you're eligible for and how to access it, from the Apprenticeship Levy to co-investment, so cost never becomes a barrier.

### Outstanding Learner Experience

From first enquiry to final assessment, learners get proactive support, clear feedback and a team that genuinely cares whether they succeed.

## Our Mission

To provide meaningful, accessible learning experiences that transform careers and strengthen organisations.

### Tutors from the trade

Apprentices are taught by people who've done the job — sector-matched tutoring that turns theory into something workable from week one.

### Built around the role, not a script

Start with the role, then choose the programme. Every enquiry begins with a skills scan against the occupational standard, so the training fits the actual job rather than a sales pitch.

### Funding handled, not just mentioned

Our programmes are 95–100% government-funded, and we manage the paperwork with you so nothing lapses mid-programme.

### A genuine job and a real wage

Apprentices earn from day one, carry no student debt, and face no upper age limit — many are adults formalising or switching careers.

### Quality that's independently checked

Our standards are verified by outsiders, not just claimed by us — external accreditation, external audit, and an independent end-point assessment at the end of every programme.

### Relationships that last

We build partnerships, not one-off contracts — when a cohort delivers, employers come back with the next one.

### Open to every starting point

Level 2 needs no formal entry requirements; our routes run all the way to Level 6, so there's a realistic step up for almost anyone.

### Support at every review

A named point of contact and off-the-job hours planned around real shift patterns keep learners progressing and the business running.
