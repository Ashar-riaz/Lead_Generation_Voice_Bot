## Frequently Asked Questions

What programmes does WTD offer?

We deliver apprenticeships from Level 2 (GCSE equivalent) to Level 6 (degree equivalent) across marketing, digital and IT, business, food and drink, butchery, AI, and engineering and manufacturing. Most programmes run 15–24 months and end with an independent end-point assessment.

Are your courses accredited?

Yes. WTD is on the Department for Education's Register of Apprenticeship Training Providers (RoATP) — the only route to delivering government-funded apprenticeship training in England — has been assessed as meeting the Expected Standard by Ofsted, and holds ISO 9001 certification.

What is an apprenticeship job?

An apprenticeship job is a genuine job with an employer — full employment rights, a real wage, holiday and sick pay, and structured training built in. Off-the-job training takes place during the apprentice's normal working hours. The minimum amount required is determined by the apprenticeship standard and is planned and agreed with the apprentice and employer as part of the training plan. It's not work experience or an internship: the employment relationship comes first, and the training is built around it.

What types of apprenticeships can you do?

You can do apprenticeships from Level 2 (GCSE equivalent) right up to Level 6 (degree equivalent). Level 2 needs no formal entry requirements; Level 3 — the most common level nationally — is equivalent to two A-levels; Levels 4 and 5 match the first years of a degree; and Levels 6 and 7 deliver a full degree with a university partner, without tuition fee debt.

Where can I find an apprenticeship?

Through the government's national apprenticeship service, or by contacting an apprenticeship training provider like WTD directly — many roles are filled before they're ever advertised publicly. We enrol every month, so there's no long wait for the next intake.

What is a government funded apprenticeship?

One where the Department for Education covers some or all of the training cost through the Growth and Skills Levy. Apprentices aged 16–21 are currently fully funded regardless of employer size, and non-levy employers contribute just 5% for older apprentices.

How do I compare apprenticeship training providers in the UK?

Check RoATP status and the current Ofsted rating, ask whether the apprenticeship training provider has delivered your specific standard before, and ask about tutor caseloads — an overstretched tutor is one of the most common causes of a poor apprentice experience.

How much will it cost my business?

Often very little. Levy funds or government co-investment cover most or all training costs, and under the 2026 reforms all apprentices aged 16–24 become fully funded for every employer from August 2026. We confirm your exact position at the free consultation.

Who is eligible for an apprenticeship?

Anyone aged 16 or over, not in full-time education, and able to work the required hours — there's no upper age limit. Higher-level apprenticeships typically expect GCSEs in English and maths or the equivalent, while Level 2 generally has no formal entry requirement at all.

Does WTD deliver apprenticeships in London?

Yes — we're headquartered at Aegon House, Lanark Square, Canary Wharf, which makes face-to-face contact easy for London-based employers. We also deliver nationwide across England, supporting clients from ArcelorMittal to the Bank of England online or on-site.

## How our training is delivered

Delivery is built to keep your operation moving while your people develop. Sessions run online or in person, new intakes open every month, and each employer has a single named contact throughout. Off-the-job learning is timetabled around your shifts, with clear progress checkpoints along the way.

**WHAT TO EXPECT**

•  One-to-one coaching alongside group sessions that bring different teams together.

•  Tutors matched to your sector, so guidance comes from someone who has done the job.

•  Focused attention on the specific gaps an apprentice needs to close.

•  A steady link between what's taught and how it's applied back at work.

•  Discussion across cohorts that mixes ages, backgrounds and points of view.

Learners stay embedded in your workplace the whole time, which keeps motivation high, before finishing on an independent end-point assessment against the national standard.
