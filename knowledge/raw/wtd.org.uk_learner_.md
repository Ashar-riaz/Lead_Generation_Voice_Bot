# Learn, Earn and Grow

Gain practical skills, earn a salary and achieve recognised qualifications while building your career.

[Find My Apprenticeship](https://wtd.org.uk/programmes/)

WTD offers a range of training solutions that can be tailored to your needs and aspirations.

## Why choose an apprenticeship?

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/sruaayi1/image/upload/v1/wtd/media-caption/home_hero.webp)

Whether you're taking your first step into work, restarting after a break, or moving into something completely new, an apprenticeship opens doors that a classroom alone can't. You build knowledge, skills and confidence in the exact field you want to work in — and you do it on the job, getting paid the whole way through.

As an experienced training provider, WTD is here to help you make the most of that. We'll build on what you already know, back you with a tutor who understands your industry, and keep you moving toward the career you're aiming for.

## So, what exactly is an apprenticeship?

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/sruaayi1/image/upload/v1/wtd/media-caption/ded.webp)

A nationally recognised programme that combines paid employment with structured training, with apprenticeship training costs supported through government funding or an employer's levy where eligibility requirements are met. You need to be 16 or over to start, but there's no upper age limit, so it's just as much a route for career-changers as for school leavers.

You'll follow an Individual Learning Plan built around your chosen qualification and job role, covering both the practical and the theory. And because you're a proper employee, you get the same treatment as anyone else on the team:

BENEFITS

•  A wage from your very first day.

•  Paid holiday entitlement.

•  Sick pay.

•  Pension contributions.

•  The same rights and benefits as your colleagues.

## What levels of apprenticeship can you do?

| Level | Name | Typical entry | Duration | Roughly equal to |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Level 2 | Intermediate | No formal entry requirements needed | 12–18 months | 5 GCSEs (grades 9–4 / A\*–C) |
| Level 3 | Advanced | Some GCSEs or a Level 2 apprenticeship | 18–24 months | 2 A-levels |
| Level 4–5 | Higher | A-levels or a Level 3 apprenticeship | 24–36 months | Foundation degree, HNC or HND |
| Level 6–7 | Degree | Foundation degree, HNC or HND | 36–48 months | A full bachelor's or master's degree |

This table shows apprenticeship levels that exist nationally, from Level 2 up to Level 6-7 degree apprenticeships. WTD currently delivers programmes from Level 2 to Level 6. Where you start depends on your experience, existing qualifications and the role itself.

## How is the training delivered?

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/sruaayi1/image/upload/v1/wtd/media-caption/wtd_helo_img.webp)

The way an apprenticeship is taught depends on the qualification and the role — some suit online or face-to-face sessions wherever you happen to be working, while others work best in a classroom or day-release setting. Whatever the format, it's chosen to fit how you learn best.

Off-the-job training takes place during your normal working hours. The minimum amount required is determined by the apprenticeship standard for your qualification, and is planned and agreed with you and your employer as part of the training plan. Your tutor comes from your own sector, so the guidance you get is grounded in the job you're actually doing.
